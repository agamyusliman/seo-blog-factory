# Content & image rules (2026-07 revision)

## Location / local SEO

| Allowed city keywords | Forbidden as case-study location |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Batam, Tanjungpinang (optional Kepri) | Nagoya, Batu Aji, Sekupang, Batam Centre, Harbour Bay, Lagoi, Tanjung Uban, Senggarang, etc. |

- City keyword = “POS SaaS available in this city”, not “merchant X in district Y uses Kekasir”.
- Hypothetical scenarios OK; fake local case studies are not.

## Images

- Style: **flat / modern brand illustration** + informative diagrams (flows, checklists, comparisons).
- Colors: navy `#1E3A8A`, orange `#EA580C`, sky `#BAE6FD`, blue `#0EA5E9`, warm `#FFFBF5` (landing DESIGN-SYSTEM).
- Section-bound: process H2 → flow chart; HPP → formula diagram; QRIS → payment flow.
- No photorealistic people; watermark still post-process from `logo-horizontal.png`.

## Keywords & angles

- Emphasize: **aplikasi kasir**, **point of sales**, **POS**.
- Prefer problem→solution articles aligned with https://kekasir.id/faq.
